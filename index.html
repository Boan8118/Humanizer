// the edit pass

Three passes, same paragraph.

It's not a black box — it's the same edits a good editor makes by hand, run automatically and tuned to a tone you pick.

01

Paste your draft

Anything with that flattened, over-hedged AI cadence. Works on paragraphs, emails, product copy, essays.

02

Casual, conversational, professional, or academic-lite — and how heavy a hand to use on the rewrite.

03

Read the markup

See exactly what changed in a diff view, copy the result, and keep a running history of every pass.

// under the hood

What it actually edits

BUZZWORD STRIP

Swaps "utilize," "leverage," "delve into," "furthermore" and the rest of the AI phrasebook for words a person would actually say.

RHYTHM VARIANCE

Breaks up uniform sentence lengths — joins some short ones, splits some long ones, so it doesn't read like a metronome.

CONTRACTION PASS

Tone-aware contractions. Casual gets loose and conversational; professional stays buttoned up.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

The free rule-based engine runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
